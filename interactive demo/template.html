Calculation

Step 4 · Show the working
  1. Answer → characteristic. Ratios are worked out from the form, exactly as in the data pipeline (e.g. loan ÷ goods price; active ÷ total credits; debt ÷ credit on active credits).
  2. Characteristic → group. The value falls into one of the coarse groups agreed in Step 5. Missing values (no bureau history, no employer, occupation not stated) have their own group.
  3. Group → WOE. Each group has a Weight of Evidence fitted on the training sample (scorecardpy sign: positive = riskier than average).
  4. WOE → points. Points = round( −Factor × coefficient × WOE + base points ÷ 11 ).
  5. Decision. Add the 11 points. Approve if the score is 600 or more.
